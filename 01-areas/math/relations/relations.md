# Definition

It is defined as the relationship between two sets. Given two sets $A$ and $B$, a relation from $A$ to $B$ associates elements of $A$ with elements of $B$; an element of $A$ may be related to several elements of $B$ or to none. When every element of $A$ is related to exactly one element of $B$, the relation is a function. Here, set $A$ is called the domain of the relation, and set $B$ is called the range of the relation.

---

Let $A$ and $B$ be sets. A binary relation defined from $A$ to $B$ is a subset of $A \times B$.

$$
R = \{(a,b) \; | \; a \in A, b \in B\}
$$

> Another notation is $aRb$.
