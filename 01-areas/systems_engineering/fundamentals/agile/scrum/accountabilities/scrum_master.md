# Scrum master

They are accountable for establishing Scrum as defined in the Scrum guide, by helping everyone understand Scrum theory and practice within the team and the organization.

## Responsibilities

- Coach and facilitate.

- Remove impediments.

- Improve effectiveness.

## How the Scrum master shows up in the Scrum events

- [[01-areas/systems_engineering/fundamentals/agile/scrum/events/sprint_planning|Sprint planning]]: facilitates the event so the team can align on what will be delivered and how. The Scrum master watches for issues like unclear backlog items, unrealistic capacity assumptions or hidden dependencies.

- [[01-areas/systems_engineering/fundamentals/agile/scrum/events/daily|Daily]]: protects the daily from turning into a long status meeting by reinforcing the goal; developers inspect progress and adjust the plan to meet the sprint goal.

- [[01-areas/systems_engineering/fundamentals/agile/scrum/events/sprint_review|Sprint review]]: facilitates collaboration between the team and stakeholders so they can review what was done and gather feedback.

- [[01-areas/systems_engineering/fundamentals/agile/scrum/events/sprint_retrospective|Sprint retrospective]]: facilitates a safe place for constructive feedback and helps the team inspect/adapt how they work. The Scrum master drives the conversation toward action, not just discussion.
