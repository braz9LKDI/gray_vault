# Product backlog

It is an emergent and ordered list of what is needed to improve the product and it is the single source of work undertaken by the Scrum team.

It contains product backlog items (PBIs) plus a product goal.

> The [[01-areas/systems_engineering/fundamentals/agile/scrum/accountabilities/product_owner|product owner]] owns it.

It helps identify the most business-critical work. Items get clarified and become "ready enough" through [[01-areas/systems_engineering/fundamentals/agile/scrum/events/backlog_refinement|backlog refinement]].
