# Definition of ready

It is a team agreement that describes the minimum conditions a [[01-areas/systems_engineering/fundamentals/agile/scrum/artifacts/product_backlog|product backlog]] item (PBI) should meet before it is selected for a sprint. Its goal is to make [[01-areas/systems_engineering/fundamentals/agile/scrum/events/sprint_planning|sprint planning]] smoother by ensuring items are understandable and actionable.

> The term "definition of ready" is **not** a required part of Scrum. A DoR is an optional practice some teams use to support transparency.
