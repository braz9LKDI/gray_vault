# Daily

It is a short, time-boxed daily event where the developers inspect progress toward the sprint goal and adapt the [[01-areas/systems_engineering/fundamentals/agile/scrum/artifacts/sprint_backlog|sprint backlog]] as needed, creating an updated plan for the next 24 hours.

It helps the team detect blockers and adjust quickly (supporting inspection and adaptation).

## Typical format

A common structure is the "3 questions":

1. What did I do yesterday?

2. What will I do today?

3. What is blocking me?
